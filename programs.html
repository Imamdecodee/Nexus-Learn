Support levels

Choose how you want to learn.

The tier model keeps the learning experience clear: more access, more feedback, and more personal support as you move up.

Basic

A focused entry point for learners who want the core curriculum and flexible access.

  • Core curriculum
  • Group cohort
  • Recorded session access
  • Large group: 20-30 learners
Most popular

Standard

A balanced path for learners who want guidance, feedback, and a recognised outcome.

  • Everything in Basic
  • Live weekly mentorship
  • Project feedback
  • Certificate
  • Small group: 10-15 learners

Premium

A high-support path for learners focused on portfolio quality, income, and opportunity.

  • Everything in Standard
  • 1-on-1 mentorship
  • Portfolio building
  • Job and internship placement support
  • Small group or 1-on-1

How it works

Learn in focused cohorts.

We use fixed cohort start dates rather than open-ended enrollment, so learners know when they are starting, what they are building, and who they are learning with.

Fixed intake

New cohorts open every 4-6 weeks, creating a clear start point and momentum.

Capped groups

Tier-based group sizes protect the quality of teaching, feedback, and mentorship.

Smart bundles

Combine complementary skills such as marketing, content, and social media for a stronger career path.

Beyond the classroom

Built for outcomes, not just attendance.

Result-based learning

Projects, feedback, and visible progress make the value of training tangible.

Community referrals

A strong learner experience creates trust, advocacy, and new opportunities.

Progressive support

Move from Basic to Premium as your goals and confidence grow.

Portfolio and career

Premium learners get focused support for portfolios, jobs, and internships.
